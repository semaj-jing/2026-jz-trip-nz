Arrival & Drive to Lake Tekapo

📍 Lake Tekapo, New Zealand

Land in Queenstown, pick up the rental car, and drive north through Central Otago and the Mackenzie Basin to Lake Tekapo, with snack and dinner stops along the way. Arrive around sunset in time for stargazing under some of the darkest skies on Earth.
