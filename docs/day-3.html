✨ Other Activities 4 ▾

Optional extras around Queenstown (Frankton) if you have time or want to swap something out.

  • Skyline Gondola

    Ride the steep gondola up Bob's Peak for sunset views over Queenstown, plus the luge and the evening Kiwi Haka cultural show.

    Good to knowGreat low-effort post-ski evening — a short drive or walk from the waterfront. Book the Kiwi Haka show ahead in peak season.

  • Queenstown Hill Time Walk

    A ~4.2km loop (2–3hrs return) climbing through pine forest to panoramic views over Lake Wakatipu and The Remarkables.

    Good to knowModerate effort — a good leg-stretch if you want more than skiing, with the best light late afternoon.

  • TSS Earnslaw evening cruise

    A vintage 1912 coal-fired steamship cruise on Lake Wakatipu, with an optional Walter Peak farm BBQ dinner.

    Good to knowRelaxed alternative to a restaurant dinner; sailings are timed, so check the schedule and book ahead.

  • Frankton Arm Walkway

    Flat, easy lakeside path right from the Frankton accommodation, following the Frankton Arm with Remarkables views.

    Good to knowVery low effort and no booking — an easy stroll before or after dinner near the accommodation.
