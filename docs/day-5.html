Flexible Day — Market, Skiing or Hiking

✨ Other Activities 3 ▾

Optional extras around Queenstown (Frankton) if you have time or want to swap something out.

  • Frankton Arm Walkway

    Flat, easy, stroller-friendly walk right from the accommodation base in Frankton, following Lake Wakatipu with Remarkables views.

    Good to knowGood low-effort swap for skiing — walk a short stretch or the full 2–3hr return, then still make it back in time for the market or dinner. No booking needed.

  • Kawarau Falls Scenic Reserve

    Short, easy stroll very close to Frankton with mountain reflections on the lake — good if time is tight.

    Good to knowMinimal time commitment (under an hour), easy to combine with the market or an early dinner.

  • Bob's Cove

    Easy/moderate lakeside walk (~3.3km) with a secret Moke Lake viewpoint if up for a steeper detour — a nice sunset option.

    Good to knowTakes a bit longer to reach than the other two, so best if skiing is skipped entirely rather than combined with a half-day on the mountain.
