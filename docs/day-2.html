Scenic Drive to Queenstown

📍 Queenstown (Frankton), New Zealand

A full day retracing part of the route back toward Queenstown, with stops at Lake Pukaki, the Clay Cliffs, Cromwell, Lake Wanaka, the Crown Range, and Arrowtown before settling into the Frankton accommodation for the rest of the trip.
