Six days chasing the light across the South Island — from the glacier-blue hush of the Mackenzie Country to the gold-and-tussock passes of Central Otago. We sleep beneath the darkest skies on Earth at Lake Tekapo, then carve four days of fresh snow off Coronet Peak and The Remarkables, with Queenstown and the lake waiting each evening below.
