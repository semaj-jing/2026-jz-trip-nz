✨ Other Activities 3 ▾

Optional extras around Queenstown if you have time or want to swap something out.

  • Frankton Arm Walkway

    A last flat, easy lakeside stroll straight from the Frankton accommodation — minutes from the airport for the departure.

    Good to knowVery low effort and no booking; ideal for the morning if you'd rather skip a final ski session before flying out.

  • Kawarau Falls Scenic Reserve

    Quick, easy walk beside Kawarau Falls next to Frankton — a scenic spot to stretch your legs before the drive to the airport.

    Good to knowUnder an hour and right on the way to the airport, so it fits even on a tight departure-day schedule.

  • Skyline Gondola (only if skipping the ski)

    If you swap the final ski session for a relaxed morning, ride the gondola for one last panorama over Queenstown and Lake Wakatipu.

    Good to knowOnly realistic if you skip skiing — leave plenty of buffer for the car return and international check-in.
